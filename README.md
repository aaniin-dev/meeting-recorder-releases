# Meeting Recorder for Mac

Records your calls and writes the transcript for you. Hold **Fn** anywhere to dictate: talk, let go, and the words
appear where your cursor is.

### [⬇ Download Meeting Recorder](https://github.com/aaniin-dev/meeting-recorder-releases/releases/latest/download/Meeting-Recorder.pkg)

1. Open the downloaded file, **Meeting-Recorder.pkg**.
2. Click **Continue**, then **Install**. Meeting Recorder opens when it's done (🎙 in the menu bar).
3. When your Mac asks, allow **Microphone**, **System Audio** and **Accessibility** (Accessibility lets Fn dictation type
   into other apps).

The first launch downloads the speech model (575 MB) once. Transcripts and dictation start working when it finishes.

**Needs:** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14.2 or later.

**Private:** recordings, transcripts and dictation stay on your Mac. The only download is the speech model, from
Hugging Face.

**Updating:** download and install the new version the same way. Your recordings, settings and permissions are kept.

All versions: [Releases](https://github.com/aaniin-dev/meeting-recorder-releases/releases).
